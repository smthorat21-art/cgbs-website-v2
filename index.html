Know Us

Community-led work, with people at its centre.

CGBS is the short name for Chhatrapati Gramvikas Bahuuddeshiya Sanstha, based in Pune, Maharashtra. This overview avoids unverified impact figures and partner claims.

Our focus

Creating opportunities for participation, awareness and community development across education, health, skills, inclusion and youth engagement.
